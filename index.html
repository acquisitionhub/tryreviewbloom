How it works

Three steps from a blank list to an audit in someone's inbox.

Search a niche and a town

Type "plumbers in Reading" or "dentists in Leeds" and get the businesses Google shows for that search.

See who's falling behind

Each profile gets a score, compared with the leaders in the same search, so the weakest prospects rise to the top.

Send the audit

Export a one-page report for the business, or a CSV for your outreach tool. On Agency and Studio, reports carry your logo.

What every audit checks

Public profile information from Google's official Places API, the same details a customer sees before they call.

Review count
How many reviews they have compared with the top results in the same search.
Star rating
Their average rating against the local leaders.
Review recency
When the latest review arrived, a quick sign of whether anyone is asking for them.
Opening hours
Whether hours are listed at all.
Website
Whether a website is linked to the profile.
Phone number
Whether customers can call straight from the listing.
Photos
Whether the profile has photos customers can browse.
Category
The primary category the business is listed under.

Questions

What does "lifetime" mean?

You pay once and keep access to your tier for as long as Review Bloom is offered, including improvements to the features in that tier. There's no monthly fee from us.

What counts as an audit?

One business profile analysed and scored. Searching a niche and town to see the list doesn't use audits; opening or exporting a report for a business does. Your allowance resets every month.

Do I need a Google API key?

Yes. You create a Google Maps Platform key and paste it in during setup. Google bills usage to your account at their rates, and we walk you through creating the key.

Where does the data come from?

Google's official Places API. Review Bloom only reads public profile information, the same details anyone sees on Google Maps. There's no scraping.

Does it work outside the UK?

Yes. Searches work wherever Google has business listings.

Can I move up a tier later?

Yes. Email us and you pay the difference between your tier and the one you want.
