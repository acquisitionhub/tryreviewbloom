FAQs

Frequently asked questions

How quickly will I see results?
Most clients see their first new reviews within 24 hours of launching their reactivation campaign. Within 7 days you'll typically have a meaningful new batch of reviews. Google Map Pack ranking improvements usually begin within 2–4 weeks depending on your starting position and competition.
Does this work with my existing software?
Yes — we integrate directly with Jobber, Housecall Pro, Workiz, QuickBooks, and thousands of other platforms via Zapier. If you use any job management or CRM software, there's a very high chance we can connect to it. Our setup call walks you through the whole process.
Is this against Google's terms of service?
No. Asking your real customers to leave a review is completely legitimate and encouraged by Google. What Review Bloom does is make that process systematic and easy — we never fake reviews or pay for them. Every review generated is from a genuine customer who experienced your service.
What if I get a negative review?
Negative reviews are rare when you're consistently delivering good service. But when they happen, our AI automatically drafts a professional, empathetic response. The best defence against one bad review is a large volume of positive ones — which is exactly what Review Bloom builds for you.
How does the review reactivation work?
You provide us with your past customer contact list. We then drip personalised SMS and email review requests to them gradually — this looks natural to Google and generates a steady flow of fresh reviews rather than an unnatural spike. Typically 20–30% of past customers who receive a request leave a review.
How does Review Bloom help with Google Map Pack rankings?
According to Whitespark's 2026 Local Search Ranking Factors report — compiled by 47 local SEO experts across 187 factors — review signals account for 20% of Map Pack rankings, up from 16% in 2023. GBP signals account for a further 32%. Combined, that's over half your Map Pack position determined by exactly what Review Bloom manages.
Can I start with GBP Management only and add reviews later?
Yes — and this is actually a smart sequence. Per Whitespark 2026, GBP signals are the single biggest Map Pack factor at 32%. Starting with our GBP Management plan (£297/mo) gets your profile fully optimised first. Then when you add review automation, every new review lands on a fully-optimised profile that amplifies its ranking impact. You can upgrade at any time with no re-setup fee.
Do you work with local businesses beyond tradespeople?
Yes — Review Bloom works for any local business that relies on Google search. Restaurants, salons, estate agents, accountants, gyms, solicitors, physiotherapists, opticians, and many more. The product is identical — automated review requests, GBP management, AI responses — applied to your specific business type and local area. We have a dedicated page for local businesses → with sector-specific details.
Do I need a website to use Review Bloom?
No — Review Bloom works with or without a website. However, having a website linked to your GBP significantly improves your Google rankings. If you don't have one, we offer a professional 5-page trade website for a one-off £497 build fee plus £47/month hosting, live within 7 days. It's available as an add-on alongside any Review Bloom plan.
